# User Preferences & App Context

## Core Constraints & Goals
- **Adaptive Cooking Environment & Effort**: Independently support diverse setups (`full_kitchen`, `microwave_only`, `microwave_mini_fridge`, `limited_kitchen`, `custom` + `"other"` equipment) and four independent effort levels (`very_low`, `low`, `moderate`, `normal`), plus an optional **Dorm / College Living** contextual profile (limited/no fridge, freezer, counter space, shared appliances, minimal cookware, shelf-stable priority, smaller quantities).
- **Scaled Ingredient & Prep Complexity**: Keep `very_low` (2–5 items) and `low` (3–6 items) effortless with accessible Walmart staples, while allowing `moderate` (4–8 items) and `normal` (5–14 items) when chosen — never forcing microwave-only into dorm mode or forcing low effort on full kitchens.
- **Self-Contained Step Instructions**: When presenting recipe steps, explicitly mention the exact ingredient name and measurement directly in the instruction so the user never has to switch back and forth to an ingredient list. Never assume kettles, hot plates, colanders, or extra appliances the user did not select.
- **Non-Judgmental Variety & Comfort Options**: Respect familiar comfort meals (chips & salsa, ramen, mac & cheese) without food shaming. Let users choose between `familiar`, `balanced`, or `new_ideas`, maintain an optional "see less often" list, and offer optional ingredient upgrades.
- **Walmart Grocery Integration & Storage Clarity**: Keep shopping lists organized by Walmart store sections (Produce, Meat & Deli, Dairy & Eggs, Pantry / Canned, Frozen, Bakery), link ingredients to their meal, and surface clear storage needs (`Shelf-Stable`, `Needs Fridge`, `Needs Freezer`) plus `Already Have` vs `Still Need` breakdowns up front.
