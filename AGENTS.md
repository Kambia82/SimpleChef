# User Preferences & App Context

## Core Constraints & Goals
- **Low Cognitive Load / Low Working Memory**: All meal suggestions, instructions, and workflows must require minimal mental bandwidth and zero culinary guesswork.
- **Strict Ingredient Limit**: Limit meals to **3 to 5 ingredients total**, using standard, accessible items available at Walmart (e.g. pre-cooked rotisserie chicken, steam-in-bag veggies, canned beans, tortillas, pre-shredded cheese).
- **Self-Contained Step Instructions**: When presenting recipe steps, each step must explicitly mention the exact ingredient name and measurement directly in the instruction so the user never has to switch back and forth to an ingredient list.
- **Rut Prevention**: Actively prevent falling back into repetitive food ruts (e.g. chips & salsa, instant ramen, boxed mac & cheese) by providing lateral, effortless upgrades that give comparable comfort with superior nutrition.
- **Walmart Grocery Integration**: Keep shopping lists organized by Walmart store sections (Produce, Meat & Deli, Dairy & Eggs, Pantry / Canned, Frozen, Bakery) and link ingredients directly to the meal they are meant for.
